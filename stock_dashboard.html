Historical Valuation Price Range
How this is calculated. Historical multiples are applied to the stock's latest available fundamentals: P/E fair value = current EPS × historical P/E; P/B fair value = current BVPS × historical P/B. EPS and BVPS are derived from the same live feed as the displayed ratios (price ÷ ratio), so inputs and history never drift apart. The median is the primary reference because it is less sensitive to extreme historical valuation periods; the average is shown for context. For banks the combined value weights P/B 60% / P/E 40%; other stocks weight both 50/50. A margin of safety (default 20%) turns the fair range into a conservative attractive-entry range. This is a relative valuation method based on the stock's own history — not a DCF or intrinsic-value calculation.
